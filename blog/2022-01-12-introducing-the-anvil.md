​The article titled _“Inverting the model of genomics data sharing with the NHGRI Genomic Data Science Analysis, Visualization, and Informatics Lab-space”_ (DOI: [10.1016/j.xgen.2021.100016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xgen.2021.100016)) introduces the NHGRI Genomic Data Science Analysis, Visualization, and Informatics Lab-space (AnVIL), a cloud-based platform designed to revolutionize genomic data sharing and analysis. Traditionally, genomic data sharing has involved downloading large datasets to local infrastructure, posing challenges in terms of data security, storage, and computational resources. AnVIL inverts this model by bringing researchers to the data within a secure, scalable cloud environment, thereby enhancing data accessibility and collaborative research efforts. The platform integrates various tools and resources, facilitating efficient data analysis while ensuring compliance with ethical and legal standards. The authors, including Jonathan Lawson, emphasize the need for policy advancements to fully leverage such technological innovations, advocating for frameworks that support secure, equitable, and efficient data sharing to drive scientific discovery.​
